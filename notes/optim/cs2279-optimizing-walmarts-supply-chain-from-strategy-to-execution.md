# Optimizing Walmart's Supply Chain from Strategy to Execution
**Walmart** · 2024 · [source](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/inte.2023.0093)

## Problem
Walmart's Every-Day-Low-Price promise depends on running one of the world's largest retail supply chains at minimal cost. The company needed decision support that spans the full horizon from strategic network design down to daily execution, rather than optimizing those layers in isolation.

## Approach / System design
Walmart built end-to-end optimization capabilities covering three connected layers: network planning and transformation for strategic decisions about the supply-chain footprint; routing and loading systems (including the Load Planner) for operational transportation decisions; and a simulation platform to evaluate decisions before they hit the physical network. Together these let strategy and execution be optimized as one integrated stack.

## Key decisions
Integrating strategic network design, transportation routing/loading, and simulation into a single optimization capability rather than separate point tools. Further design detail is not covered in the accessible portion of the source.

## Stack
Network design optimization, vehicle routing and load-building systems, and a supply-chain simulation platform. Specific solver and infrastructure details are not covered in the accessible portion of the source.

## Results
The work won the 2023 INFORMS Edelman Award. Reported impact includes $91.5M in FY23 savings, elimination of 108K truck routes, and 98.6M lbs of CO2 emissions prevented.

## Takeaways
Treating supply-chain optimization as a continuum from strategy to execution — with simulation as the bridge — lets savings compound across layers: better network design creates routing opportunities, and routing/loading systems realize them daily at Walmart scale.
